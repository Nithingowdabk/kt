Overview

Kudremukh is a mountain range and name of a peak located in Chikkamagaluru district, in Karnataka, India. It is shaped like a horse's face and is the second-highest peak in Karnataka. The trek takes you through lush green shola forests, bamboo shrubs, and multiple water streams.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 0
Midnight Rollout – Departing Bangalore Under the Stars

Depart from Bengaluru at 9:00 PM. Overnight journey in a pushback bus.

Day 1
Into the Wild – Trek to Peak

Arrive at the homestay in Chikkamagaluru by 6:00 AM, freshen up, and enjoy a traditional breakfast. Start trekking through misty paths.

Day 2
Scenic Sightseeing After Trek & Return

Wake up, enjoy breakfast, visit local sightseeing places, and depart back to Bengaluru.
